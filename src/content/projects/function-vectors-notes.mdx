## What the intervention can show

A function vector is useful when injecting it changes a model's behavior under a
specified experimental setup. That result supports a causal role for the
intervened activation in that setup. It does not establish that the model has a
clean internal category, or that the same vector will transfer across models,
layers, prompts, or evaluation rules.

## Evidence required for a case

Each published case needs to record:

- the model and revision;
- the layer, component, and intervention location;
- the source heads or other selection rule;
- the demonstrations and evaluation probes;
- the scoring rule and baseline;
- the evaluation split or seed; and
- a run artifact that connects those settings to the displayed outputs.
